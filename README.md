## 👋 About Me

I am a Full-Stack Software Engineer specializing in distributed systems, scalable architectures, and reliable end-to-end product delivery. Currently at Kigo — [@erika-amastal](https://github.com/erika-amastal) — I drive comprehensive integrations and feature development across the entire application ecosystem.

My engineering approach bridges complex technical challenges with business needs, focusing on high performance, maintainability, and clear execution across the stack.

- **🔧 End-to-End Engineering:** I take full ownership of the product lifecycle. My work ranges from provisioning new cloud infrastructure and architecting robust backends using Domain-Driven Design (DDD), to integrating complex new features across both frontend and backend for the Kigo app.
- **🏗️ Scalable Architecture:** Focused on building event-driven, high-availability systems using Go, TypeScript, Python, and AWS/Kubernetes that remain fast and consistent under heavy loads.
- **👥 Technical Leadership:** Experienced in mentoring engineers and establishing best practices. I have led technical teams at Cruz Roja Puebla (Django microservices) and SYNTAX (Cloud operations center for 2,000+ servers), driving collaborative and high-quality delivery.
- **🎯 Product-Minded Ownership:** I align technical decisions with real-world user needs and business objectives to ship features that drive measurable impact.

📍 Based in Puebla, México — building software that scales.
